## Method notes

ATR(14) is the mean true range over the last 14 daily candles ending 2026-08-12,
where true range is `max(high−low, |high−prev close|, |low−prev close|)`,
expressed as a percentage of the 08-13 last price. Position in the 52-week range
is `(last − 52wk low) ÷ (52wk high − 52wk low)`. All inputs came from
`get_quotes` (verbose) and `get_advanced_price_history` on 2026-08-13; none of it
is recalled from memory. The catalyst sleeve is deliberately **not** screened
here — it needs a post-print gap to react to, and the first qualifying reports
land 08-18 onward.
